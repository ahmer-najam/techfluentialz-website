Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about app development

Most projects take 4-6 months from conception to launch. Simple apps may be completed in 2-3 months, while complex apps with extensive features might take 8-12 months.

We recommend starting with your target audience's primary platform. Many successful apps start with one platform and expand. Cross-platform development with React Native or Flutter can be cost-effective for reaching both markets.

Costs vary widely based on complexity, features, and design. Simple apps start around $10K, while complex enterprise apps can exceed $100K. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your requirements.

We provide ongoing maintenance, bug fixes, updates for OS versions, and feature enhancements. This ensures your app remains secure, performant, and competitive.

We implement industry best practices including secure authentication, data encryption, secure API communication, and regular security testing to protect user data.
